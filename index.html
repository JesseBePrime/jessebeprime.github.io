About

Designing at the boundary between hardware and control

I am completing an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Utah, where I conduct wearable-robotics research in the HGN Lab for Bionic Engineering.

I enjoy carrying projects from kinematic modeling and CAD through fabrication, electronics integration, control implementation, and experimental testing. I am especially interested in robotic actuators and systems that interact directly with people.

  • SolidWorks and Autodesk Fusion
  • MATLAB and Simulink
  • C and embedded control
  • Sensor and motor integration
  • PCB design in Autodesk Eagle
  • Prototype testing and analysis
