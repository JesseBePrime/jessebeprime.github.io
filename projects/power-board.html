Electronics design

High-voltage power-management board

A compact PCB that accepts the high-voltage battery bus used by robotic motors while providing a separate regulated supply for embedded electronics.

Role
Schematic capture, layout, and manufacturing preparation
Requirements
Up to 80 V input and a 30 V, 2 A electronics output
Tools
Autodesk Eagle and PCBWay
Electrical requirements

One battery bus must serve motors and lower-voltage electronics

Wearable robots often combine high-voltage DC motors with control hardware that operates at a lower voltage. The system needed a small board that could route battery power to the motor subsystem and step the same input down for the onboard electronics.

The design accepts an input of up to 80 V and provides a 30 V output rated for 2 A to the electronics branch.

Board architecture

Separate connections make the power paths clear at integration

The layout distinguishes the battery input, motor power output, and regulated electronics output. Connector placement and the compact component arrangement support integration inside the limited volume available in a wearable robotic system.

Implementation

Schematic and layout developed as one manufacturing package

I created the circuit schematic and physical board layout in Autodesk Eagle, then prepared the design for outside PCB manufacturing. This project expanded my work beyond mechanical design into the power electronics required to operate an integrated robot.
